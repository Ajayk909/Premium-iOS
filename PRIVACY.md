# Premium — Privacy Policy

Premium is a personal YouTube client for iOS.

- **Google sign-in is optional.** When you sign in, Premium requests read-only access to your YouTube account (`youtube.readonly`) only to import your subscriptions, playlists and liked videos into the app.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Imported data and your watch history are stored locally on your iPhone. Your sign-in token is kept in the iOS Keychain.
- **No servers, no tracking, no ads.** Premium has no backend of its own, does not collect analytics, and never sells or shares your data.
- **Sign out any time** from Settings in the app, or revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
